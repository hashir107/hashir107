## How I approach AI & Data products

**Understand the problem → Find the leverage → Design the system → Start small → Evaluate → Learn → Scale**

Start with the **customer problem, not the model**. I look at the whole system in terms of users, workflows, data, software, physical interfaces, and operational constraints and choose the simplest solution that creates meaningful value. That reflects my existing of customer focus, systems thinking, simplicity, and acting despite uncertainty.

I test the riskiest assumptions early and evaluate the product across **value, usability, feasibility, viability, reliability, trust, cost, and operational impact**.

**The goal is not to add AI. It is to build intelligent systems that solve real problems and work reliably in practice.**
